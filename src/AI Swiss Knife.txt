Problem
Files are scattered across emails, PDFs, forms, and multiple systems that don’t talk to each other. Adjusters spend hours cross-checking data and re-entering information just to open, reserve, or pay a claim. The process is slow, inconsistent, and audit-heavy, draining time and money while frustrating customers. Claims represent the largest cost line for insurers yet many workflows remain manual, error-prone, and outdated. The result: long delays, high variability, poor customer experience, and lost efficiency at scale.
Solution:
Our solution uses AI agents to streamline claims processing. The system reads and extracts data from multiple sources (emails, documents, and core systems) and executes routine tasks automatically (open claims, validate coverage, reserve/pay). Every action is logged to create a transparent, auditable trail that meets compliance requirements. Instead of wasting time on repetitive work, claims adjusters focus only on exceptions and high-value decisions. This reduces cycle times, lowers costs, and improves consistency while delivering faster, clearer outcomes for customers and regulators alike.

Market/Competition

With 20–25% efficiency gains achievable through AI-driven claims automation, the addressable savings reach €8–10.5B annually.
